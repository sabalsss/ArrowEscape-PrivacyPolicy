Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape: Shape Puzzle (the "App") by SabalApps (the "Developer").
Effective date: October 24, 2026

Arrow Escape: Shape Puzzle is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting services. The App does not request internet access, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (picture puzzles completed, stars, revealed shapes, daily puzzle results, statistics) and your settings (such as sound and reminder preferences) are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on the optional daily puzzle reminder, the App asks for notification permission. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device. You can turn it off in the App's settings or in your device's system settings at any time.

Google Play services

If you are asked to rate the App, the App may use Google Play's In-App Review feature. This is provided and operated by Google, and Google's handling of any information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App does not receive your review content or personal details from this flow. Downloading the App through Google Play is also governed by Google's terms and policies.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the App opens your device's share sheet with a short message and a link to the App's Google Play page. What you send, and to whom, is up to you; the App does not see it.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share or disclose.

Data security and retention

Since all data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We keep no copy of it and cannot access it.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above changed. Please review it from time to time.
